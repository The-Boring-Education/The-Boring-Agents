# Project Idea: An app that enables you to find Flats to host you temporarily

## Description

-   Imagine you're living in Mumbai for 3 Years and get bored. You want to live in Kolkata for 1 or More months. But if you visit Kolkata, you'll pay rent for a Mumbai Flat and a New Kolkata Flat. Why not host someone at the Mumbai flat?

-   Imagine you're a Remote Worker and want to visit Pune in monsoon, and you open our app, which says 15 people are allowing you to visit Pune for 1 Month. You be their guest - Pay the rent and live in their flat while they visit another city.

_Who doesn't want to live in KOLKATA DURING DURGA PUJA? Everyone does._
